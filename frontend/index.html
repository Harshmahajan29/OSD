Stop 1: the curriculum

The spellbook

One day, two deep-dive sessions. The morning takes you from what open source is to how microservice systems are designed, connected and deployed. The afternoon makes you comfortable with Git and GitHub, from your first commit to rebasing.

Microservices

Session 1

Architecture & APIs

Client-server model, RESTful API design, and monolith vs microservices.

Distributed Internals

Database-per-service, Redis caching, and Kafka / RabbitMQ message queues.

Deployment & Live Project

Docker, Kubernetes orchestration, and an end-to-end project walkthrough.

Git & GitHub

Session 2

Linux & VCS Basics

Terminal navigation and version control fundamentals for project tracking.

Git Core Workflow

Working tree, staging area, commit history, and ignoring untracked files.

Branching & Collaboration

Branch isolation, merging, conflict resolution, and remote teamwork on GitHub.

Stop 4: about us

Meet the team

Walchand Linux Users' Group (WLUG) is the open source club of Walchand College of Engineering, Sangli. We are students who use, build and promote Linux and open source software, and we have been spreading it around campus since 2003.

Our goal is simple: make open source technology approachable for every student, through hands-on sessions, club services and events that take you from your first terminal command to real-world tools.

  • Since 2003promoting open source at WCE
  • 3 amazing eventsLinux Diary, Open Source Day and Metamorphosis

Stop 5: questions

Ask the crystal ball

Who can attend?

Students from any year and branch who are curious about open source.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Sessions start from the basics, and the hands-on part walks you through every step.

Is there a registration fee?

No, Open Source Day is completely free.

What should I bring?

A laptop and charger if you have one. If not, PCs will be available at the venue.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Everyone receives a digital certificate at the email address they registered with.

Stop 6: events we conduct

The events we conduct
